**Hi, I'm Crystal!** The main hostess of the brand new MediaPlay newsletter here on the official website of BFM and also on Patreon!

MediaPlay is the all-new newsletter where I review what's going on in the world of digital entertainment, gaming, and new stuff related to indie animations that has been going around in the center of the web and on-demand through streaming platforms.

We have a lot to check out in the future as we dig deep into what's hot across the communities around your favorite content and, of course, look at what's coming soon for the content you want to see!
