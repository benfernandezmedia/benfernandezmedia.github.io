Hi everyone, Benjamin and Amira here to give you a heads-up about the upcoming projects we’re planning to get back to working on! One of those projects specifically is none other than the **Enhanced** series of illustrations, as two illustrations that are part of the series were completed since the beginning of the year.

Last month, during the late weeks prior to the official website launch, I was considering returning to working on the series since I have not been drawing throughout April, but as the summer season is coming up soon, and of course, the upcoming season of ConnectiCon XXIII later this July, more illustrations that are older than 2021 or earlier will be redone with a new look.

Now, I want to turn it over to Amira and see what she has to say in regards to Enhanced. **Take it away, Amira!**

## Amira's Headline

Thanks, Benjamin! Hey guys, Amira here! Excited to do my first post on the website along with my creator. As he has said before, Enhanced is returning with more illustrations later this month as older illustrations will be planned to work on, and some illustrations I want to focus on mentioning in particular are the ones made for our friends over at **Team Pyro**, which consists of the artist duo of Pyropen Art and Bunny's Resin Arts.

As you may or may not have noticed, April marked the month when we launched our Benjamin Fernandez Media Instagram account, one of our social media channels. The account began with the original illustration titled “Love Is Slippery,” which was inspired by a video our friend Pyro created for Valentine’s Day two years ago on his social media platforms. This video also played a significant role in the inception of our online presence on Instagram, showcasing what we do regarding content and the art we post!

### Team Pyro x Enhanced

This is the first time that the illustrations we did for both Pyro and Bunny are going to be fused through the world of Enhanced, and needless to say, it's going to be a blast to see these two creative artists be re-done in newer versions straight on Procreate!

We continue to maintain our friendship with the duo and continue to support them throughout their lives and their art journey online and in-person through the conventions.

### New original illustrations for Enhanced

Even though Benjamin is looking back at his old catalog of illustrations, new original illustrations will be coming to the series, and for the first time, our new friend Madeline will also join in the line-up, showing off her interactions with us and her character too! So stay tuned on our socials and even on our site for more updates related to the series. We also have an **RSS Feed** so you can add us into your feeder so you can check for updates faster instead of heading to the site manually!

That's all I have to announce, let me turn back to Benjamin as he has updates in regards to the site!
