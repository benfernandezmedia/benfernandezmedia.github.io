*First illustration with a Nintendo character? Well...this is good to be true in many ways!*

<!--more-->

I am so lucky that I had only one fair share of creating illustrations based off the games that I play in own spare time but the good thing is that this happens at rare times since I mostly focus on my universe. That’s why last night, after listening to the Swapnote soundtrack two or three times, I decided to draw Nikki, who is the software’s mascot, in my own art style.

While I originally intended to draw it my way, it didn’t fit right due to how I draw the eyes for Nikki, so I decided to keep it original and Nintendo-ish in a way so I won’t have to take time on adding some specific details on her.

The artwork will be later published today on Instagram and on Cara. Go check it out by clicking on the links below to see it there!
