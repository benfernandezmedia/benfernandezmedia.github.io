## The Review of the Finale

![The Amazing Digital Circus](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/benfernandezmedia/mediaplay-database/refs/heads/main/mediaplay-issue02/tadclastact.png)

As the box office has gotten GLITCH Productions worldwide with The Last Act screening of both Episodes 8 and 9, the finale has fully premiered on both YouTube and Netflix. By looking at the poster art and trailer for the finale, this episode is primarily focused on Jax and takes place after the events of the previous episode when Kinger accidentally purged Caine from the circus.

The reason this finale focuses on Jax is because he has a complicated past in his life at the circus and a friendship with Ribbit, one of the former circus members before she was abstracted. Plus, this is also the first time we see Kaufmo, before his abstraction, in the episode, as he was first mentioned in the pilot episode. Based on what I noticed in the finale, we didn't actually see Jax abstracted with our own eyes at the first few minutes of the episode, as this was technically an off-screen moment.

Although, throughout the series, Jax's behavior and actions are basically his misdemeanor of screwing around with the other circus members, in the finale, we get to see Jax's vulnerability and personal conflict at the same time.

Despite of Kaufmo, Ribbit, and Jax's downfall that led to their abstraction, the surviving members of the digital circus finally remembers their lives before the circus and also **their real names** after when Caine returned to allow them to have full control of the circus itself and live their lives to the fullest.

Aside, from the animation, this is also the first time that live action was used in a show by GLITCH as the post-credits revealed the real life versions of the main characters, which is very impressive.

## The Media Verdict for the Finale

As the series ended with nine episodes in total, and also that the finale was mostly emotional for the characters including Jax's final moments, me and the MediaPlay team gives the final episode of *The Amazing Digital Circus* with a rating of 9 out of 10 with an A grade.

There was one criticism I have in regards to the abstraction of Jax at the ending of the finale, **why didn't we saw him abstract early in the episode?** Probably, that would been explanatory due to his mental state worsening throughout the series up until this point in the finale.

But at least the gang has their happy ending...*especially Zooble and Gangle having their make out moment* during the credits of the show, which should not be discussed in the workplace if you know what I mean.
