## Returning To The Nostalgia With A New Pilot

Ah yes, the early 2000s...the millennium...we knew the vibes of early childhood were returning, but with an indie twist too! Last Monday, the fans and viewers of indie animation were stoked with a new pilot episode from artist and creator, **Rafael Andaya**, with their new show called [^4]*PLANETRONIKA*, an early-Y2K sci-fi comedy featuring two cool-looking characters that have been on the buzz throughout the week. What we have here is *a sneak preview video of the pilot episode! **Take a look!***

### Plot of the PLANETRONIKA Pilot Episode

Taking place on a fictional planet called Tereste with its early Y2K vibes, two friends named Butterfly and Freestyle decided to take a break from work and challenge each other with a game of basketball! Unfortunately, their usual game gets chaotic with a dribble malfunction as the ball goes haywire in their surroundings.

It's not usual for a plot like this where a game of basketball gets deadly after one malfunction, but in the world of PLANETRONIKA, it takes a go of a misadventure with a big twist of millennium fun!

### The pilot episode is filled with classic memes and references

*Well then... it seems like this show has a lot of well-known memes and references from the early-2000s internet back in the day.* For those who were born as Generation Z (people who were born in 2000), you may recognize some memes and references throughout the pilot episode, like the *split-second troll face, the three guys on a planet (which I do not know the name of), and, of course, the Caramelldansen dance* performed by Butterfly. *Have you found them all in the episode?*

### The Media Verdict for PLANETRONIKA

*Ayeiyaiya, I'm your little butterfly~ Oh, sorry! I zoned out a bit there. Y2K will always be embedded in the Generation Z DNA.* Anyway, since the show had demonstrated the vibes of old-fashioned Y2K shows based on its look and feel on the world-building, characters, plot, and setting, *MediaPlay* gives **Rafael Andaya's PLANETRONIKA** an 11 out of 10 with an average scale of A+! The extra point was given since a lot of detail and remembrance of nostalgia were put in during the making of the pilot episode!

## Video Games Are Everything...But In A Deadly Twist

![Gameoverse](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/benfernandezmedia/mediaplay-database/refs/heads/main/mediaplay-issue01/gameoverse.webp "Official cover art for Gameoverse featuring Kit and other characters.")

While [^2]**GLITCH Productions** is focusing on stuff related to the final episode of *The Amazing Digital Circus* and its theatrical debut for the premiere of both Episodes 8 and 9 across the world this summer, they have released a new show called *Gameoverse* that was created and directed by **Ross O'Donovan**, while **Arin Hanson**, who is famously known online as the co-host of *Game Grumps* on YouTube, co-written the show. This action-packed adventure consists of the idea of where video games meet the apocalypse, as each game world gets destroyed if that world doesn't get saved in time.

As the show was released on Friday last week, that has been reports that fans were starting to see a bit of *fan service* from the episode since it was actually a beach episode, and then there was some backlash over the design choices for both Kit and Miss Information on numerous posts on Twitter.

Although the show looked pretty good, and the character designs and world-building have been put in a lot of hard work by the team over at GLITCH.

![TADC](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/benfernandezmedia/mediaplay-database/refs/heads/main/mediaplay-issue02/tadclastact.png)

*What!? MediaPlay has started its first issue, and now we have breaking news already over The Amazing Digital Circus? Man, that was pretty fast!*

## The Fans of Helluva Boss Got Chaz'd...once again

![Sharkboy Summer](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/benfernandezmedia/mediaplay-database/refs/heads/main/mediaplay-issue01/helluvaboss_short12.webp "Official YouTube thumbnail for the Helluva Boss short titled 'Sharkboy Summer' ")

*I'm almost done here on this first issue, but now we are entering VivziePop territory* as Friday is also marked with a new release of a 12th short, or basically a music video from SpindleHorse's show, *Helluva Boss.* Originally, the social channels of [^3]SpindleHorse posted an image of what fans had assumed that the next short would involve, Verosika Mayday, due to the appearance of a character shown from an underwater perspective at a local pool.

![TwitterPost](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/benfernandezmedia/mediaplay-database/refs/heads/main/mediaplay-issue01/twitterpost.png "The initial Twitter post in regards to the release date of the short.")

However, that all changed when **Eric Schwartz**, the voice of Chaz, whose character had recently appeared in the April Fools short and in the earlier episodes of the show, posted on social media, sharing a hint that it's not Verosika while he was swimming in a pool.

So it appears that Chaz or Zahc or *whatever the F@#K his name is*, was most likely to appear again for the new short. On the day of the shorts' release, the entire HB fandom got flabbergasted by a new music video titled "Sharkboy Summer".

*In all honesty, I have confirmed in my suspicions that Chaz is NOT dead in a way, although he had his teeth ripped by Moxxie's father in one of the episodes, which has been revealed in the previous short, and has shark dentures.* But we will never know whether it's **actually** him or his so-called twin brother, nor whether he is actually DEAD, that may not stop him from his game of being the most perverted character throughout the entire series. But that's just my opinion in a way, not to be disrespectful to the show's VAs and the main creator.

All I can say is that the funeral was basically a setup so that Chaz could fool everyone and get away with it, and probably gonna be continuing bragging about his big appendage. Obviously, that is the context of the music video, as this is *technically* a song of Chaz's seductive territory. But to put this positively, it's also to note that we get to see the Envy ring for the very first time. In all strong honesty, most people, including myself, had fair shares of seeing weird things around the internet and mostly stuff in the show, but this was *WAYYYYYY* overboard, so the short was **too much Chaz**, *if you know what I mean.*

Overall, aside from that seductive weirdo, we are still looking forward to the upcoming seasons of Helluva Boss and also the new seasons of Hazbin Hotel from SpindleHorse, which will be coming soon on YouTube and on Amazon Prime Video. Please continue to follow the social channels of SpindleHorse for more updates and details regarding the Helluvaverse.
