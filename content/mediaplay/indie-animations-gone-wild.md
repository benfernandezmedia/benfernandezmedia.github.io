## Returning To The Nostalgia With A New Pilot

Ah yes, the early 2000s…we knew the vibes of early childhood is returning but with an indie twist too! Last Monday, the viewers of indie animation has been stoked with a pilot episode from artist and creator, Rafael Andaya, with his new show called *PLANETRONIKA*, a sci-fi, early-Y2K comedy, featuring two cool-looking characters that has been the buzz throughout the week. Here is a preview of the pilot episode!

### Plot of the PLANETRONIKA Pilot Episode

Taking place in a fictional planet with its early Y2K vibes, two friends named Butterfly and Freestyle decided to take a break from work and challenge each other with a game of basketball! However, their usual game get chaotic with a dribble malfunction as the ball goes haywire.

With that plot, it’s not the usual for a game of basketball to go haywire but in the world of PLANETRONIKA, it takes a go of a misadventure with a big twist of the millennium fun!

### It’s filled with references and memes

For those who are Generation Z (people who are born in the Y2K), you may recognized some memes throughout the entire episode like the split second troll face, those three guys on an airplane, and even Butterfly doing the Caramelldansen dance! Have you catch them all?

### The Media Verdict for PLANETRONIKA

Since the show demostrated the vibes of old-fashioned Y2K shows based on its look and feel on the world-building, characters, plot, and setting, MediaPlay gives Rafael Andaya's PLANETRONIKA pilot episode an 11 out of 10 with average grade of A+! The extra point was given due to the fact that a lot of detail and remembrance of nostalgia was put in during the making of the pilot episode!
